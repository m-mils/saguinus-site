Known issues

Saguinus is still in live testing, so a few rough edges remain. Here is what's currently known and being worked on:

  • PDF export's paper size is currently always A4, even if you choose Letter or Legal in the Page menu — the live editor and on-screen preview do follow your choice, but the exported PDF doesn't yet.
  • Switching the export preview to "Continuous" mode doesn't yet produce a single unbroken PDF page — it currently exports the same multi-page PDF as "Paged" mode.
  • Exported PDF pages can come out very slightly shorter than what you see in the in-app preview — content isn't lost, just occasionally shifted by a line or two near a page break.
  • On Linux, exporting to PDF currently crashes the app. This is a confirmed bug in Electron itself (not Saguinus), and a fix is pending upstream — Windows is unaffected.
