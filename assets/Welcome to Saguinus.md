## Working With Math

Inline math fits right into a sentence, like the quadratic formula\
$x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$, without breaking your flow.

Block equations stand on their own:

$$
E = mc^2
$$

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$
